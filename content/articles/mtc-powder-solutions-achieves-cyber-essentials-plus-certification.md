#### **MTC Powder Solutions Achieves Cyber Essentials Plus Certification**

Independent assessment confirms that MTC Powder Solutions’ IT environment meets the requirements of the Cyber Essentials Plus scheme.

MTC Powder Solutions is proud to announce that it has achieved Cyber Essentials Plus certification, marking an important milestone in the company’s ongoing work to maintain a secure digital workplace.

Cyber Essentials Plus is an independently verified cybersecurity standard. The assessment reviewed MTC Powder Solutions’ devices, software, user access, security updates and supporting processes. The certificate covers the whole organisation and confirms that, at the time of testing, its IT defences met the scheme’s requirements for protection against common cyberattacks.

Certification is one part of MTC Powder Solutions’ wider approach to information security. Cyber threats continue to evolve, and the company will maintain its focus on improving systems, strengthening safeguards and supporting secure working practices across the organisation.

MTC Powder Solutions thanks all colleagues for their patience, cooperation and commitment throughout the implementation and assessment process.

## Certification details

**This certification supports MTC Powder Solutions’ commitment to protecting its people, customers, partners and business information.**
