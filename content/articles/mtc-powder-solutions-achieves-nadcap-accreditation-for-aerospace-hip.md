# MTC Powder Solutions Achieves Nadcap Accreditation for Aerospace HIP



## Advancing our aerospace quality commitment

MTC Powder Solutions has achieved **Nadcap accreditation** for Hot Isostatic Pressing **(HIP)**, strengthening our ability to support aerospace programmes governed by demanding process-control, traceability and quality-assurance requirements.

The accreditation follows the successful completion of the PRI evaluation process and our Nadcap audit. It provides independent recognition of the systems, process discipline and technical controls applied within our approved scope. This milestone strengthens our HIP service offering for the aerospace industry and reflects the standards pursued across MTC as a group.

 

## What this means for our customers

For aerospace customers requiring Nadcap-approved special processes, the accreditation provides formal recognition that MTC Powder Solutions has successfully completed the PRI evaluation process for the services covered by our approved scope. It gives customers added confidence in process control, pyrometry, documentation, traceability and the consistent execution of HIP operations.

 

The accreditation also supports our ambition to grow our HIP Service business in aerospace markets where repeatability, objective evidence and disciplined process management are central to customer approval. It creates a stronger basis for supporting current programmes, new qualifications and long-term customer partnerships.

 

## A shared achievement built by our people

Reaching this milestone required commitment across every department and role at MTC Powder Solutions. Quality, Technical, Business Development, HIP operations and company management worked together through audit preparation, internal audits, document review, training, implementation and follow-up. Every completed action, challenged assumption and recorded improvement helped build the result.

Our thanks go to  everyone[](https://www.office.com/search?q=Daniella+Tern+Nyberg&EntityRepresentationId=b305a90d-526a-8d2c-b8cd-0f138d3749dd) who contributed time, knowledge and focus. This achievement belongs to the people who prepared the evidence, strengthened the procedures, operated the equipment, answered the audit questions and supported colleagues throughout the process. Nadcap accreditation is not an endpoint. It sets a platform for continuous learning, stronger skills and sustained attention to the standards expected in aerospace. By learning from each audit, each production cycle and each customer requirement, we develop our people and reinforce the quality culture behind every HIP cycle.
